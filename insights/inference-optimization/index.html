What was measured

Asari reports up to 16% improvements in throughput and interactivity for DeepSeek v4 Pro and GLM 5.2 on eight NVIDIA B200 GPUs, against matched vLLM v0.23 baselines.

Source

How candidates were evaluated

The reported loop combines profiling, implementation, performance evaluation, and statistical checks on token-level output distributions. Agents reuse experimental findings to guide subsequent work.

Source

How to interpret the result

The comparison applies to the published workloads and concurrency levels. Distribution checks provide evidence within the evaluation’s scope; they do not prove every possible output correct or establish savings for another deployment.
